FANAAR LOGISTICS - FREE INCOMING EMAIL FORWARDING
Updated: 23 October 2026

Company address: info@fanaarlogistics.com
Domain provider: Porkbun

WHAT THIS DOES
Customers email info@fanaarlogistics.com. Porkbun forwards each message to
an existing inbox you choose. It does not create a separate mailbox.
Replies from that receiving inbox show its own address.

CREATE THE FORWARD
1. Sign in at https://porkbun.com/.
2. Open ACCOUNT, then Domain Management.
3. Find fanaarlogistics.com and click the envelope in its EMAIL column.
4. On Email Hosting and Forwarding, find Porkbun Email Forwarding.
5. Under Create a New Forwarding Address, enter:
   Username: info
   Forwarding destination: your existing receiving email address.
6. Click Create Email Forward.
7. Confirm Current Forwards lists info@fanaarlogistics.com and the right
   destination. A DNS record alone does not create this forwarding rule.
8. Test from a DIFFERENT email account than the receiving inbox. Check
   Inbox and Spam in the destination account.

CURRENT DNS CHECK
The domain's MX records currently point to fwd1.porkbun.com and
fwd2.porkbun.com. This confirms the mail-server routing, not whether an
info forwarding rule or its receiving inbox has been configured.
Do not change the website's DNS records for this forwarding procedure.

WEBSITE CONTACT LINK
The website's company email is maintained in src/config.js as CONTACT.email.
A mailto link opens the visitor's email application to compose a message.
Publish website changes through the documented GitHub Pages workflow.

PORTAL EMAIL IS A SEPARATE SETUP
Porkbun forwarding receives customer mail. It does not authorize the
Fanaar portal to send updates or PDF invoices. Outgoing portal mail needs
a configured sending service and verified company sender. Staff Google
sign-in currently grants identity access only, not Gmail sending access.
Until that connection is configured, use a prepared email draft or
download the invoice PDF and attach it in your existing email app.
