tracking/quote page destinations to configure. Do not paste a password,
shared admin token or Resend secret into config.js or any VITE_ variable.

On the backend, PUBLIC_ORIGIN must match the actual customer origin. On the
current Worker origin, Access protects staff pages/assets and /api/admin/*;
only approved exact customer paths bypass staff login. Before releasing the
private completion form, add the exact /api/bookings/details exception and
allow its Authorization and Idempotency-Key request headers in CORS. That
endpoint still verifies its private bearer token; it is not a public record
lookup. Never bypass /api/admin/* or the whole Worker hostname. Staff APIs
still require verified identity and active D1 membership. See the backoffice guide;
do not weaken these checks to fix a browser error.

  npm ci
  npm run dev

Open the printed loopback address, normally http://127.0.0.1:5173.
For a local backend preview only, set the value before starting Vite:

  $env:VITE_SERVICE_API_URL = 'http://127.0.0.1:8787'

The backend's separate local environment must also be running. Its test
identity is only for loopback development; never publish or tunnel it.
Use synthetic data. Never publish a frontend pointing at localhost.

For a production-style local build with confirmed live backend addresses:

  $env:VITE_SERVICE_API_URL = 'https://api.fanaarlogistics.com'
  $env:VITE_ADMIN_LOGIN_URL = 'https://admin.fanaarlogistics.com'
  npm run build
  npm run preview

terminal overrides when finished:

  Remove-Item Env:VITE_ADMIN_LOGIN_URL -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue

9. WHAT CUSTOMERS AND STAFF CAN DO

Customer quotes preserve the original enquiry details: required name,
phone, cargo type, origin and destination; optional company, email, weight
with kg/tons/lbs, volume and message. The friendlier form adds service
preference, package quantity, dimensions, value, pickup/delivery addresses,
preferred date and up to three package photos.

For the QUOTE stage, pickup and delivery full addresses are OPTIONAL. A
customer can request pricing with city/island endpoints without knowing the
full collection address yet. The first step makes optional address boxes
easy to find; the last step reviews them and provides Edit locations. Do
not make these quote address boxes required when editing the form.

Current uploads are JPEG/PNG/WebP photos only. Each prepared upload must
be at most 1 MiB; input photos over 15 MiB are rejected and accepted photos
are resized. PDF and Office documents are not supported in this first
photo-only version. Photos are private staff attachments, not tracking data.

After a genuine API success the customer sees a quote reference. Staff
review the quote, agree the price, mark it accepted and create the shipment.
The shipment gets a separate unpredictable tracking number. Staff share it
with the customer. Public tracking is by that code, not by a phone number.
In shipment detail, Copy tracking link copies the full main-website URL;
Link copied confirms success. Paste it into a manual customer reply. This
does not send an automatic email or WhatsApp message.

Second stage: complete pickup and delivery details
  This feature is published. After agreeing the quote and creating the booked
  shipment, staff may issue the operational invoice and create a private
  customer completion link from that shipment. Staff review the recipient
  before sharing it manually. The link expires after seven days and can
  also be revoked; create a fresh link when necessary.

  The customer opens the link on the main Fanaar website and sees a popup
  with the quote/booking reference, invoice number if present, route and
  service. An invoice/reference number by itself cannot open the form.
  The secret token is in the URL fragment, is removed from the address bar
  after opening and stays only in page memory. Do not put private links in
  screenshots, public issues, analytics or the public repository.

  At this BOOKING stage, full pickup and delivery addresses are REQUIRED,
  each including area/ward, street/building and a useful landmark. Contact
  name and phone at BOTH ends are also required. Preferred collection date
  and time window are optional. If a date is supplied, it must be today or
  later in Tanzania. Customers can review and submit with both timing fields
  blank. Any supplied date/time is a request, not a guaranteed appointment;
  Fanaar will contact the customer to confirm arrangements. Delivery notes
  remain optional. Customers review all details, explicitly confirm, then send.

  Submission is one-use. The same retry key safely retrieves its receipt;
  reopening a used link does not expose stored addresses or phone numbers.
  Corrections go through staff. Staff can fill the same details in the
  private shipment record. Collection/status advancement requires complete
  booking details. Quote enquiries and public tracking remain separate.

Staff can review requests; assign private carriers, contacts and departures;
record the private vessel name on the relevant departure;
record pickup/ETA/delivery; add public progress; record payments; and print
operational invoices. The portal has an owner role for Settings and Staff
access, with normal Staff handling the operational work. New staff also need
their matching Google email manually added to the Cloudflare Access allow
policy before login; portal staff changes do not synchronize that allowlist.

Private supplier pricing and costs
  Supplier rates stores carrier prices by route, service, optional cargo type
  and unit: per shipment, package, kg, ton or cubic metre. Confirm the current
  carrier rate yourself. These private reference cards do not calculate a
  customer quote or automatically fill a shipment's costs.

  In a quote, staff may record an estimated carrier and estimated carrier
  cost. The estimate's displayed margin is customer quoted amount less that
  carrier cost only; pickup and other expenses are excluded. The customer
  price is entered and agreed manually, not generated from the rate card.

  Shipment supplier costs have three separate categories: carrier, pickup
  and other direct costs. Blank means unknown. Enter 0 only when there is
  truly no charge; unknown must not be replaced with zero to produce a total.
  Amounts are nonnegative whole TZS. All three must be known before the full
  direct-cost total is shown.

  The customer amount for comparison is the issued invoice subtotal BEFORE
  TAX, or the current quote amount when no invoice exists. The screen's
  Gross margin is that amount less the three recorded direct costs. This is
  shipment contribution before overheads/unrecorded expenses, not net profit.
  It stays unknown if the price or a cost is missing. Payments collected and
  the invoice total including tax are different figures.

  Dashboard supplier costs include only shipments with complete direct-cost
  records; its margin total also needs a known customer amount. The missing
  cost count shows records excluded from these partial totals. They are not
  complete company accounts. Rates, estimates and supplier margins remain
  private and do not appear in public tracking or customer invoices.

The database is the record of actual submissions. With no backend connection,
a form error is not a saved request. Phone/WhatsApp remain useful alternatives.

10. EMAIL, INVOICES AND BUSINESS SETTINGS

The manual email/PDF tools are already published. Resend Free is configured,
fanaarlogistics.com and its sending DNS records are verified, and the
restricted sending key is stored as an encrypted backend secret. It is not
part of the public website source or build. The deployed sender is
Fanaar Logistics <info@fanaarlogistics.com>. No email has been sent: provider
acceptance, inbox receipt and emailed PDF attachments are not yet live-tested.
Configured does not mean delivery has been demonstrated.

Resend Free allows 100 transactional emails per UTC day and 3,000 per month.
The daily allowance resets at midnight UTC (03:00 Tanzania time). Each
recipient counts separately, and any Resend inbound mail shares the quota.
Paid transactional overages remain off. Keep the reviewed Free plan and
check provider usage rather than assuming unlimited sending.

In the staff portal, choose Email customer update or Email invoice. Review
From, Replies to, To, subject and the read-only message; for invoices also
review the attached PDF. Explicitly confirm before Send update or Send
invoice. The portal accepts invoice PDF attachments up to 512 KiB and keeps
private supplier costs, margins and internal notes out of customer PDFs.
Saving a shipment status does not send a customer email. Existing held
messages are not released by configuring a sender. SMS and automated
WhatsApp sending are not included.

Email accepted for delivery means the provider accepted the send request,
not proof of delivery, inbox placement or reading. Even a provider delivered
event means acceptance by the recipient's mail server, not proof of inbox
placement/read. Queued or sending is pending; use Check status before any
retry. Failed approved messages have a separate reviewed retry action.
Preview readiness checks configuration, not actual provider delivery.

Staff can Download invoice PDF, Prepare PDF to share and then Share PDF, or
Open customer WhatsApp. Native sharing lets staff choose an app and recipient;
if unsupported, use the downloaded file. The WhatsApp chat link fills text
only: attach the downloaded PDF manually. Open email draft cannot attach
the PDF automatically; download and attach it in the email app. That draft
uses the sender account selected in the operator's email app. Opening a
draft, chat or sharing menu does not send or confirm delivery in the portal.

The reply inbox is still awaiting the owner's choice. Without a separate
reply address, customer replies fall back to info@fanaarlogistics.com; its
receiving inbox/forwarding has not been verified. A sending domain does not
create a receiving mailbox. Confirm the destination and test incoming mail
separately using Fanaar-Email-Forwarding.txt. Preserve existing MX records.
Forwarding receives mail; it does not provide authenticated outgoing domain
mail. Do not assume replies will arrive until incoming delivery is checked.

Fill in real business email, address and payment instructions in backoffice
Settings before issuing invoices. Prices/payments are whole TZS shillings.
Do not invent tax IDs or rates. The printable document is an operational
invoice; legal/fiscal suitability needs the business's own review. Issued
invoices retain their recorded issuer/customer details. Use a deliberately
approved controlled inbox to verify the sender, reply destination, tracking
link and PDF attachment before relying on the email flow for customers.

  After the customer confirms receipt, staff mark Delivered and review the

  The default alert is 90%, adjustable by an Admin or Owner. It estimates this app's
  recorded attachment bytes, including pending uploads/deletion, not the whole account's
  actual/billable usage. Check Cloudflare metrics too. Alerts are not billing
  caps; email alerts require separate provider configuration.

  The cleanup UI is deployed; actual cloud cleanup still needs its separately

  tables to make the dashboard smaller. Save authorized copies first if
